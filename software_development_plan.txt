**Cursor‑Powered MVP Development Plan**
*Leveraging Cursor AI as your coding assistant throughout every phase.*

---

## 1. MVP Goals

1. **Core Interaction:** Touch‑driven dot‑to‑dot tracing with next‑dot highlighting and undo.
2. **AI Coaching:** Friendly voice prompts after each successful line.
3. **Template Flexibility:** Three starter templates (smiley, child’s face, superhero mask).
4. **Invite‑Only Access:** Simple email‑link or token gate.
5. **Polish & Metrics:** Progress indicator and basic usage logging.

## 4. How to “Vibe Code” with Cursor

* **Inline Prompts**: In your VS Code‑Cursor editor, type natural prompts above empty functions or stubs and accept suggestions.
* **Iterative Edits**: Ask Cursor to refactor or optimize (e.g., “Make this draw loop debounce for performance”).
* **Test Generation**: Prompt “Create simple unit tests for dot‑to‑dot ordering logic.”
* **Documentation**: Use Cursor to scaffold short README sections for each phase.

---

## 5. Success Criteria

* **Children** can tap through all dots in order and undo.
* **Voice prompts** play within 1 s after each line.
* **Three templates** load and switch seamlessly.
* **Only invited** testers can access the app.
* **Progress** displays and basic session logs exist.
